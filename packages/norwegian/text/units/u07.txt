## lesson Big and small
p adj-agree | Describing words change | needs: gender, plural
rule: A describing word matches its noun: en stor bil, et stort hus, store biler.
rule: Add -t for et words and -e for more than one. The same happens after er: huset er stort.
ex: en stor bil | a big car
ex: et stort hus | a big house
ex: store biler | big cars
ex: huset er stort | the house is big
q: et nytt hus | *a new house | new houses
q: fine bøker | *nice books | a nice book
w stor | adj | big
w liten | adj | small (with en words)
f lite | adj | small (with et words) | lemma=liten
f små | adj | small (more than one) | lemma=liten
w ny | adj | new
w fin | adj | nice

## story Ei ny jakke | A new jacket
Nora: Jeg trenger ei ny jakke. Denne er for liten. | I need a new jacket. This one is too small.
Ida: Hvilken farge vil du ha? | Which colour do you want?
Nora: Jeg liker blå klær. | I like blue clothes.
Ida: Se, denne jakka er blå. Og fin! | Look, this jacket is blue. And nice!
Nora: Ja, men hva koster denne? | Yes, but how much is this one?
Ida: Jakka er veldig dyr. | The jacket is very expensive.
Nora: Har de en annen butikk i nærheten? | Is there another shop nearby?
Ida: Ja, ved stasjonen. Klærne der er billige. | Yes, by the station. The clothes there are cheap.
? Why does Nora need a new jacket? | *her jacket is too small | her jacket is too expensive | she has no jacket
? Where are the clothes cheap? | *in the shop by the station | in this shop | at the hotel

## story Broren til Marco | Marco's brother
Marco: Dette er broren min. Han er høy, ikke sant? | This is my brother. He is tall, isn't he?
Emma: Ja! Og han ser snill ut. | Yes! And he looks kind.
Marco: Han er snill og veldig morsom. | He is kind and very funny.
Emma: Har han barn? | Does he have children?
Marco: Ja, to små jenter. De er fire og seks år gamle. | Yes, two small girls. They are four and six years old.
Emma: Og huset? Er det huset hans? | And the house? Is it his house?
Marco: Nei, det er huset vårt i Italia. Det er gammelt, men fint. | No, it is our house in Italy. It is old, but nice.
? How many children does the brother have? | *two | four | six
? Whose house is it? | *Marco's family's | the brother's | Emma's
