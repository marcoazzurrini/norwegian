# Forms of words that UniMorph lacks or has wrong: word | class | form=what form=what ...
# The word itself is the infinitive of a verb, the singular of a noun, the masculine singular of anything else.
# What a form is: p1 to p6 (yo, tú, él, nosotros, vosotros, ellos), pl, fem, m-pl, f-pl.
# A word that never changes has an empty list.
