# unit 10 | Planer | Plans, weather and invitations

## lesson Going to
p future | Going to | needs: modal
rule: For a plan, use skal plus the verb with no ending: jeg skal reise.
rule: With a time word, the present is enough: jeg reiser i morgen.
ex: jeg reiser i dag | I am travelling today
ex: jeg skal reise i morgen | I am going to travel tomorrow
ex: hva skal du gjøre? | what are you going to do?
q: vi skal spise ute | *we are going to eat out | we are eating out
q: skal du jobbe? | *are you going to work? | are you working?
w skal | verb | going to | lemma=skulle | only
c neste uke | next week
w neste | det | next
w sommer | noun en | summer
w vinter | noun en | winter
w ute | adv | outside

## lesson It is raining
p det-subj | It rains, it is cold | needs: present
rule: Weather and general statements start with det, like English "it": det regner.
rule: The describing word takes -t after det: det er kaldt, det er varmt.
ex: det regner | it is raining
ex: det er kaldt | it is cold
ex: det er fint vær | the weather is nice
q: det snør | *it is snowing | that is snow
q: det er varmt i dag | *it is warm today | he is warm today
w regne | verb | to rain
w snø | verb | to snow
w sol | noun ei | sun
w vind | noun en | wind
w skinne | verb | to shine

## story Helga på ski | The weekend on skis
+ ski | skis
Kari: Hva skal du gjøre i helga? | What are you going to do at the weekend?
Ola: Jeg vet ikke ennå. Og du? | I do not know yet. And you?
Kari: Det skal snø, og jeg har lyst til å gå på ski. Vil du komme? | It is going to snow, and I feel like going skiing. Do you want to come?
Ola: Det høres bra ut! Men jeg har ikke ski. | That sounds good! But I do not have skis.
Kari: Broren min har ski. Du kan bruke dem. | My brother has skis. You can use them.
Ola: Tusen takk. Når skal vi dra? | Thank you very much. When are we going to leave?
Kari: Tidlig på lørdag. Vi ses klokka åtte! | Early on Saturday. See you at eight o'clock!
? What are they going to do? | *go skiing | go to the cinema | swim
? What is the problem? | *Ola has no skis | it is going to rain | Kari is busy

## story Bursdag | Birthday
Emma: Jonas har bursdag neste uke. Skal vi kjøpe en gave? | It is Jonas's birthday next week. Shall we buy a present?
Lars: Ja. Hva liker han? | Yes. What does he like?
Emma: Han liker film og fotball. | He likes film and football.
Lars: Vi kan gi ham to billetter til kino. | We can give him two tickets to the cinema.
Emma: Det er en god gave! Skal han ha fest? | That is a good present! Is he going to have a party?
Lars: Ja, på fredag. Jeg gleder meg til festen. | Yes, on Friday. I am looking forward to the party.
Emma: Jeg kan dessverre ikke komme på fredag. Jeg skal jobbe. | Unfortunately I cannot come on Friday. I am going to work.
? What is the present? | *two cinema tickets | a football | a film
? Why can Emma not come? | *she is going to work | she is ill | she is travelling
