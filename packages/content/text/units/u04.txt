## lesson More than one
p plural | More than one | needs: gender
rule: Most nouns add -er: en bil, to biler. Nouns ending in -e add -r: ei jente, to jenter.
rule: Short et words do not change: et hus, to hus.
ex: en bil > to biler | two cars
ex: ei jente > to jenter | two girls
ex: et hus > to hus | two houses
q: tre stoler | *three chairs | the chair
q: mange barn | *many children | a child
w to | num | two
w tre | num | three
w mange | adj | many | only
w noen | det | some, any
w hund | noun en | dog
w katt | noun en | cat

## lesson Mine and yours
p possessive | Mine and yours | needs: definite
rule: The owner comes after the noun, and the noun takes "the": bilen min, "my car".
rule: Min and din change with the noun: bilen min, boka mi, huset mitt. Hans and hennes never change.
ex: bilen min | my car
ex: boka mi | my book
ex: huset mitt | my house
ex: bilen hans | his car
q: huset ditt | *your house | my house
q: boka hennes | *her book | his book
w min | det | my
w din | det | your (one person)
w hans | det | his
w hennes | det | her, hers (owner)

## lesson All of them
p plural-def | "The" for more than one | needs: plural, definite
rule: To say "the" for more than one, add -ene: bilene, jentene, husene.
rule: A few common nouns change their vowel: ei bok, bøker, bøkene.
ex: biler > bilene | the cars
ex: jenter > jentene | the girls
ex: hus > husene | the houses
q: stolene | *the chairs | chairs
q: bøker | *books | the books
f bøker | noun | books (more than one bok) | lemma=bok
f brødre | noun | brothers | lemma=bror

f menn | noun | men | lemma=mann

## story Familien min | My family
Emma: Dette er familien min. | This is my family.
Emma: Mora mi heter Kari og faren min heter Per. | My mother is called Kari and my father is called Per.
Jonas: Har du søstre eller brødre? | Do you have sisters or brothers?
Emma: Ja, jeg har to brødre og ei søster. | Yes, I have two brothers and a sister.
Jonas: Hvor gammel er søstera di? | How old is your sister?
Emma: Hun er åtte år gammel. Brødrene mine er studenter. | She is eight years old. My brothers are students.
Jonas: Bor dere sammen? | Do you live together?
Emma: Nei. Brødrene mine bor i Bergen. | No. My brothers live in Bergen.
? How many brothers does Emma have? | *two | three | one
? Where do the brothers live? | *Bergen | Oslo | with Emma

## story Hunden og kattene | The dog and the cats
Ola: Har dere en hund? | Do you have a dog?
Maria: Ja, vi har en hund og tre katter. | Yes, we have a dog and three cats.
Ola: Tre katter! Liker hunden kattene? | Three cats! Does the dog like the cats?
Maria: Nei, men kattene liker hunden. | No, but the cats like the dog.
Ola: Hva heter hunden deres? | What is your dog called?
Maria: Han heter Tom. Han er ti år gammel. | He is called Tom. He is ten years old.
Ola: Sønnen min vil også ha en hund. | My son also wants a dog.
? How many cats does Maria have? | *three | two | ten
? Who wants a dog? | *Ola's son | Maria | Ola's wife
