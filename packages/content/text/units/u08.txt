# unit 8 | I går | Talking about the past

## lesson Yesterday
p past | The past | needs: present
rule: For the past, most verbs swap -r for -et or -te: snakker > snakket, spiser > spiste.
rule: The form is the same for everyone. Common verbs have their own past form, learned as words.
ex: jeg snakker > jeg snakket | I spoke
ex: vi spiser > vi spiste | we ate
ex: hun jobber > hun jobbet | she worked
q: de kjøpte brød | *they bought bread | they are buying bread
q: jeg venter | *I am waiting | I waited
c i går | yesterday
w da | adv | then (at that time)
w før | prep | before
c for ___ siden | ___ ago
c i forrige uke | last week

## story En tur til fjellet | A trip to the mountains
Jonas: Hva gjorde du i helga? | What did you do at the weekend?
Emma: Vi dro til fjellet. Vi gikk en lang tur. | We went to the mountains. We went for a long walk.
Jonas: Var været fint? | Was the weather nice?
Emma: Nei, det var kaldt. Men vi hadde det gøy. | No, it was cold. But we had fun.
Jonas: Hvor bodde dere? | Where did you stay?
Emma: Vi bodde på et lite hotell ved sjøen. | We stayed at a small hotel by the lake.
Jonas: Jeg var hjemme. Jeg leste og sov. | I was at home. I read and slept.
Emma: Det er også fint! | That is nice too!
? How was the weather? | *cold | warm | they do not say
? Where did Emma stay? | *in a small hotel by the lake | at home | at a friend's place

## story Jeg glemte nøkkelen | I forgot the key
Per: I går kom jeg sent hjem. Og jeg hadde ikke nøkkelen! | Yesterday I came home late. And I did not have the key!
Kari: Hva gjorde du da? | What did you do then?
Per: Jeg satt foran døra og ventet. | I sat in front of the door and waited.
Kari: Kom kona di hjem? | Did your wife come home?
Per: Ja, klokka ti. Hun så{verb} meg og sa: Hva gjør du her? | Yes, at ten o'clock. She saw me and said: What are you doing here?
Kari: Og hva svarte du? | And what did you answer?
Per: Jeg sa: Jeg glemte nøkkelen på jobb. | I said: I forgot the key at work.
? Where was the key? | *at work | in the car | at home
? When did Per's wife come home? | *at ten o'clock | very late at night | she was at home
