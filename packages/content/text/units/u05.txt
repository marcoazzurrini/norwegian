## lesson Verb second
p v2 | The verb comes second | needs: present, neg
rule: The verb is always the second piece of a statement.
rule: If you start with a time or a place, the person moves behind the verb.
ex: jeg jobber nå | I am working now
ex: nå jobber jeg | now I am working
ex: i dag spiser vi fisk | today we are eating fish
q: i dag jobber hun | *today she is working | is she working today?
c i dag | today
w så | adv | then, so
w ofte | adv | often
w alltid | adv | always

## story Dagen min | My day
Lars: Når står du opp, Nora? | When do you get up, Nora?
Nora: Jeg står opp klokka seks. Først trener jeg. | I get up at six o'clock. First I exercise.
Lars: Klokka seks! Jeg sover til klokka åtte. | Six o'clock! I sleep until eight o'clock.
Nora: Etterpå spiser jeg frokost og leser litt. | Afterwards I eat breakfast and read a little.
Lars: Når begynner du å jobbe? | When do you start working?
Nora: Klokka ni. Jeg slutter klokka fem. | At nine o'clock. I finish at five o'clock.
Lars: Og i kveld? | And tonight?
Nora: I kveld lager jeg mat og hører på musikk. | Tonight I am cooking and listening to music.
? When does Nora get up? | *six o'clock | eight o'clock | nine o'clock
? What does Nora do first? | *she exercises | she eats breakfast | she reads

## story Helg | Weekend
Ida: Har du fri på lørdag? | Are you off work on Saturday?
Jonas: Ja, jeg har fri hver helg. | Yes, I am off work every weekend.
Ida: Vil du komme til middag? | Do you want to come to dinner?
Jonas: Ja takk! Når? | Yes please! When?
Ida: Klokka sju. Broren min kommer også. | At seven o'clock. My brother is coming too.
Jonas: Bra. Jeg kjenner ikke broren din. | Good. I do not know your brother.
Ida: Han er vanligvis hjemme i Bergen, men i helga er han her. | He is usually at home in Bergen, but at the weekend he is here.
? When is the dinner? | *Saturday at seven | Sunday at seven | Friday at six
? Where is Ida's brother usually? | *Bergen | Oslo | at Ida's home
