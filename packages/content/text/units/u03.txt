## lesson Can, will, must
p modal | Can, will, must | needs: present
rule: After vil, kan and må, the next verb has no ending: jeg kan snakke.
rule: These three never change and never take -r.
ex: jeg snakker norsk | I speak Norwegian
ex: jeg kan snakke norsk | I can speak Norwegian
ex: hun vil spise | she wants to eat
ex: vi må drikke vann | we must drink water
q: du må komme | *you must come | you are coming
q: han vil ha kaffe | *he wants coffee | he has coffee
w vil | verb | want to | lemma=ville | only
w kan | verb | can | lemma=kunne | only
w må | verb | must | lemma=måtte | only
w ha | verb | to have
w spise | verb | to eat
w drikke | verb | to drink

## lesson To do
p inf-aa | To do something | needs: modal
rule: Å is "to" before a verb: å spise, å snakke.
rule: Use it after verbs like liker and trenger. Do not use it after vil, kan, må.
ex: jeg liker å spise | I like to eat
ex: jeg vil spise | I want to eat
ex: hun trenger å spise | she needs to eat
q: vi liker å snakke | *we like to talk | we want to talk
q: jeg må spise | *I must eat | I like to eat
w å | other | to (before a verb)
w like | verb | to like
w lage | verb | to make
w kjøpe | verb | to buy
w betale | verb | to pay
w trenge | verb | to need

## story På kafé | At a café
Ida: Hei! Hva vil du ha? | Hi! What would you like?
Tom: Jeg vil gjerne ha en kopp kaffe, takk. | I would like a cup of coffee, thanks.
Ida: Vil du ha ei kake også? | Do you want a cake too?
Tom: Nei takk, men jeg er sulten. Har dere brød? | No thanks, but I am hungry. Do you have bread?
Ida: Ja, vi har brød med ost. | Yes, we have bread with cheese.
Tom: Bra. Kan jeg få brød med ost og et eple? | Good. Can I have bread with cheese and an apple?
Ida: Ja. Vær så god. | Yes. Here you are.
Tom: Tusen takk. Hva koster det? | Thank you very much. How much is it?
? What does Tom drink? | *coffee | tea | water
? What does Tom not want? | *cake | bread | an apple

## story Middag | Dinner
Kari: Jeg er sulten. Hva spiser vi til middag? | I am hungry. What are we eating for dinner?
Per: Jeg vet ikke. Liker du fisk? | I do not know. Do you like fish?
Kari: Ja, jeg liker fisk. Har vi fisk? | Yes, I like fish. Do we have fish?
Per: Nei. Jeg må kjøpe fisk og brød. | No. I must buy fish and bread.
Kari: Kan du kjøpe melk også? Barnet trenger melk. | Can you buy milk too? The child needs milk.
Per: Ja. Og jeg vil lage ei kake. | Yes. And I want to make a cake.
Kari: Bra! Jeg kan lage kaffe. | Good! I can make coffee.
? What must Per buy? | *fish, bread and milk | cake and coffee | only fish
? Who needs milk? | *the child | Kari | Per
