# unit 2 | Hjemme | Things, people and "the"

## lesson A and an
p gender | En, ei, et | needs: present
rule: Every noun is an en word, an ei word or an et word. All three mean "a".
rule: Learn each noun with its word: en bil, ei bok, et hus.
ex: en bil | a car
ex: ei bok | a book
ex: et hus | a house
q: et hus | *a house | the house
q: ei bok | *a book | books
w det | pron | it
w har | verb | have / has | lemma=ha | only
w hus | noun et | house
w bil | noun en | car
w bok | noun ei | book
w dette | pron | this (pointing at a thing)

## lesson Saying the
p definite | Saying "the" | needs: gender
rule: Norwegian puts "the" on the end of the noun.
rule: en words add -en, ei words add -a, et words add -et.
ex: en bil > bilen | the car
ex: ei bok > boka | the book
ex: et hus > huset | the house
q: huset | *the house | a house
q: en stol | *a chair | the chair
q: døra | *the door | a door
w her | adv | here
w der | adv | there
w ligge | verb | to lie (be somewhere)
w stå | verb | to stand
w på | prep | on
c det er ___ | there is ___

## story Hvor er nøkkelen? | Where is the key?
Kari: Per, hvor er nøkkelen? | Per, where is the key?
Per: Jeg vet ikke. Ligger nøkkelen på bordet? | I do not know. Is the key on the table?
Kari: Nei. Boka og telefonen ligger på bordet. | No. The book and the phone are on the table.
Per: Er nøkkelen på kjøkkenet? | Is the key in the kitchen?
Kari: Nei, ikke der. | No, not there.
Per: Unnskyld! Jeg har nøkkelen her. | Sorry! I have the key here.
Kari: Bra! Takk. | Good! Thanks.
? Where is the key? | *Per has it | on the table | in the kitchen
? What is on the table? | *the book and the phone | the key | money

## story Et rom i Oslo | A room in Oslo
Tom: Jeg har et rom i Oslo nå. | I have a room in Oslo now.
Anna: Bra! Hva har du i rommet? | Good! What do you have in the room?
Tom: Jeg har ei seng, et bord og en stol. | I have a bed, a table and a chair.
Anna: Har rommet et vindu? | Does the room have a window?
Tom: Ja, og vinduet er veldig bra. | Yes, and the window is very good.
Anna: Bor du med en venn? | Do you live with a friend?
Tom: Ja, han heter Lars. Han er student. | Yes, his name is Lars. He is a student.
? Who does Tom live with? | *a friend called Lars | Anna | a teacher
? What does Tom not mention? | *a kitchen | a bed | a table
