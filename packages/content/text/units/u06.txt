## lesson Me, you, them
p obj-pron | Me, you, him, her | needs: present
rule: After a verb or a small word like med or til, the person changes form.
rule: jeg > meg, du > deg, han > ham, hun > henne, vi > oss, de > dem.
ex: jeg ser deg | I see you
ex: du ser meg | you see me
ex: hun bor med ham | she lives with him
q: han hjelper oss | *he helps us | we help him
q: kan du se dem? | *can you see them? | can they see you?
w meg | pron | me
w deg | pron | you (after a verb, one person)
w ham | pron | him
w henne | pron | her (after a verb)
w oss | pron | us
w dem | pron | them
w hjelpe | verb | to help

## lesson Do it!
p imperative | Telling someone to do it | needs: present
rule: Take the verb and remove the final -e: vente > vent!, komme > kom!
rule: Short verbs stay the same: gå > gå!, se > se!
ex: å vente > vent! | wait!
ex: å komme > kom! | come!
ex: å se > se! | look!
q: kom hit! | *come here! | he comes here
q: vent litt | *wait a little | waiting a little

## story Hvor er stasjonen? | Where is the station?
Tom: Unnskyld, kan du hjelpe meg? Hvor er stasjonen? | Excuse me, can you help me? Where is the station?
Kari: Stasjonen er ikke langt. Gå rett fram til banken. | The station is not far. Go straight ahead to the bank.
Tom: Og så? | And then?
Kari: Ta til venstre ved banken. Stasjonen ligger bak hotellet. | Take a left by the bank. The station is behind the hotel.
Tom: Tusen takk. Når går toget til Bergen? | Thank you very much. When does the train to Bergen leave?
Kari: Jeg vet ikke, men det går ofte. | I do not know, but it goes often.
Tom: Bra. Jeg må kjøpe en billett først. | Good. I must buy a ticket first.
? Where is the station? | *behind the hotel | in front of the bank | far away
? What must Tom do first? | *buy a ticket | eat | take the bus

## story Til flyplassen | To the airport
Anna: Vi må kjøre til flyplassen nå. Flyet går klokka ti. | We must drive to the airport now. The plane leaves at ten o'clock.
Per: Vent litt! Jeg kan ikke finne billettene. | Wait a little! I cannot find the tickets.
Anna: Se på bordet. | Look on the table.
Per: Nei, de ligger ikke der. | No, they are not there.
Anna: Har du dem i bilen? | Do you have them in the car?
Per: Ja! De ligger i bilen. Kom! | Yes! They are in the car. Come!
Anna: Bra. Kan du gi meg nøkkelen? Jeg vil kjøre. | Good. Can you give me the key? I want to drive.
? Where are the tickets? | *in the car | on the table | at the airport
? Who wants to drive? | *Anna | Per | nobody
