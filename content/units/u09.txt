# unit 9 | Har gjort | Have done, because and that

## lesson Have done
p perfect | Have done | needs: past
rule: Har plus a verb form in -et or -t: jeg har snakket, jeg har spist.
rule: Use it as in English, for something that matters now or has no stated time.
ex: jeg snakket med ham i går | I spoke with him yesterday
ex: jeg har snakket med ham | I have spoken with him
ex: har du spist? | have you eaten?
q: hun har kjøpt en bil | *she has bought a car | she bought a car
q: vi har ikke spist | *we have not eaten | we did not eat
w allerede | adv | already
w ennå | adv | yet, still
c noen gang | ever
w nettopp | adv | just (a moment ago)

## lesson Because and that
p sub | Because, that, if | needs: present, neg
rule: At, fordi and hvis join a second sentence to the first.
rule: After them comes the person, then the verb: fordi jeg er trøtt.
ex: jeg sover | I am sleeping
ex: jeg sover fordi jeg er trøtt | I am sleeping because I am tired
ex: hun sier at hun kommer | she says that she is coming
q: vi spiser hvis du kommer | *we eat if you come | we eat when you come
q: han tror at det er dyrt | *he thinks that it is expensive | he knows that it is expensive
w at | conj | that (joining two sentences)
w fordi | conj | because
w hvis | conj | if
w synes | verb | to think (have an opinion)
w hvorfor | adv | why

## story Hos legen | At the doctor's
Lege: Hei. Hvorfor har du kommet i dag? | Hi. Why have you come today?
Tom: Jeg føler meg dårlig. Jeg har vondt i hodet. | I feel unwell. I have a headache.
Lege: Har du spist i dag? | Have you eaten today?
Tom: Nei, jeg har ikke spist ennå. Jeg er ikke sulten. | No, I have not eaten yet. I am not hungry.
Lege: Jobber du? | Do you work?
Tom: Ja, jeg har jobbet veldig lenge hver dag. Sjefen sier at det er viktig. | Yes, I have worked very long every day. The boss says that it is important.
Lege: Jeg tror at du er trøtt. Du må bli hjemme og sove. | I think that you are tired. You must stay at home and sleep.
Tom: Heldigvis har jeg fri i morgen. | Luckily I am off work tomorrow.
? What is wrong with Tom? | *he has a headache | he has a cold | his foot hurts
? What does the doctor tell Tom to do? | *stay at home and sleep | eat more fish | work at home

## story Ny på kontoret | New at the office
Maria: Hei, jeg er ny her. Er denne stolen ledig? | Hi, I am new here. Is this chair free?
Lars: Ja, selvfølgelig. Har du møtt sjefen ennå? | Yes, of course. Have you met the boss yet?
Maria: Nei, hun er opptatt. Jeg prøver igjen snart. | No, she is busy. I will try again soon.
Lars: Hvorfor har du kommet til Norge? | Why have you come to Norway?
Maria: Fordi kjæresten min bor her. Og jeg synes at Norge er et fint land. | Because my boyfriend lives here. And I think that Norway is a nice country.
Lars: Du snakker bra norsk! Hvor har du lært språket? | You speak good Norwegian! Where have you learned the language?
Maria: Jeg har studert norsk i Italia. Men jeg har ikke brukt språket ofte. | I have studied Norwegian in Italy. But I have not used the language often.
? Why did Maria come to Norway? | *her boyfriend lives there | only for the job | to study
? Where did Maria study Norwegian? | *in Italy | in Norway | at the office
