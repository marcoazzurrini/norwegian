# unit 1 | Hei! | Greetings and saying who you are

## lesson I and you
p present | Verbs now | needs:
rule: A verb has one form for everyone: jeg er, du er, vi er.
rule: Most verbs end in -r in the present: snakker, kommer, heter.
ex: jeg er | I am
ex: du er | you are
ex: jeg heter Anna | my name is Anna
q: du heter | *you are called | I am called
q: jeg er | *I am | you are
w jeg | pron | I
w du | pron | you (one person)
w er | verb | am / is / are | lemma=være | only
w hete | verb | to be called
c jeg heter ___ | my name is ___
c hva heter du? | what is your name?

## lesson Saying not
p neg | Saying "not" | needs: present
rule: Put ikke straight after the verb.
rule: Norwegian does not use "do": jeg snakker ikke means "I do not speak".
ex: jeg snakker norsk | I speak Norwegian
ex: jeg snakker ikke norsk | I do not speak Norwegian
ex: du er ikke fra Oslo | you are not from Oslo
q: jeg bor ikke i Oslo | *I do not live in Oslo | I live in Oslo
q: du kommer ikke | *you are not coming | you are coming
w ikke | adv | not
w snakke | verb | to speak
w norsk | adj | Norwegian (language)
w engelsk | adj | English (language)
w forstå | verb | to understand
w litt | adv | a little

## lesson Asking
p q-yn | Yes or no questions | needs: present
rule: Start with the verb. Then comes the person.
rule: Again, no "do": Snakker du norsk?
ex: du snakker norsk | you speak Norwegian
ex: snakker du norsk? | do you speak Norwegian?
ex: er han fra Bergen? | is he from Bergen?
q: bor du i Oslo? | *do you live in Oslo? | you live in Oslo
q: kommer hun? | *is she coming? | she is coming
p q-wh | Questions with a question word | needs: q-yn
rule: Question word first, then the verb, then the person.
ex: hvor bor du? | where do you live?
ex: hva heter hun? | what is her name?
ex: hvem er han? | who is he?
q: hvor bor de? | *where do they live? | do they live there?
w hva | pron | what
w hvem | pron | who
w hvordan | adv | how
c hvordan går det? | how are you? (how is it going)
c bare bra | just fine

## story På toget | On the train
Anna: Hei! Jeg heter Anna. Hva heter du? | Hi! My name is Anna. What is your name?
Tom: Hei, jeg heter Tom. | Hi, my name is Tom.
Anna: Hvor kommer du fra? | Where are you from?
Tom: Jeg kommer fra England, men jeg bor i Oslo. | I come from England, but I live in Oslo.
Anna: Snakker du norsk? | Do you speak Norwegian?
Tom: Ja, litt. Jeg forstår litt norsk. | Yes, a little. I understand a little Norwegian.
Anna: Jeg er lærer. Er du student? | I am a teacher. Are you a student?
Tom: Ja, jeg er student. Hyggelig å møte deg! | Yes, I am a student. Nice to meet you!
? Where does Tom live? | *Oslo | England | Bergen
? What is Anna's job? | *teacher | student | she does not say

## story Hvem er hun? | Who is she?
Ola: Hvem er hun? | Who is she?
Kari: Hun heter Maria. Hun kommer fra Italia. | Her name is Maria. She comes from Italy.
Ola: Bor hun i Norge? | Does she live in Norway?
Kari: Ja, hun bor i Bergen. | Yes, she lives in Bergen.
Ola: Snakker hun norsk? | Does she speak Norwegian?
Kari: Ja, og hun snakker også engelsk. | Yes, and she also speaks English.
? Where is Maria from? | *Italy | Norway | England
? Which languages does Maria speak? | *Norwegian and English | only English | only Norwegian
